01 · The idea

The big picture

Developers send AI requests to the Infera cloud. The cloud picks an idle device that can answer, the device streams the answer back, and the device owner earns a share of what the developer paid. When no device fits, the request goes to rented GPUs so the customer is always served.

02 · System design

Architecture

Two halves: the control plane runs in the cloud in three regions, and the node app runs on each contributor's device. Devices always dial out to the cloud over a secure connection on port 443, so contributors never touch router or firewall settings.

Control plane services are written in Go. Postgres holds accounts and the ledger, Redis holds live device state, and Cloudflare provides DNS, protection and file storage.

Cloud serviceWhat it does
API GatewayTakes customer requests in the OpenAI format, checks API keys and credits, counts tokens, and bills through Stripe.
SchedulerChooses a device that has the right model loaded, is fast enough and has good reputation. Retries elsewhere if a device drops. Sends to rented GPUs when no device fits.
Node hubHolds the open connection to every device and tracks its state: idle, busy, paused, model loaded, speed.
VerifierSends test prompts with known answers and re-runs a few percent of jobs on a second device to catch bad results.
LedgerStores each job's signed receipt and turns it into points, earnings and payouts.
Push serviceSends job-offer notifications to phones (Firebase for Android, Apple Push for iPhone) and passes the owner's Approve or Reject back to the scheduler.
Model CDN and update serverServe signed model files and signed app updates, so devices only ever run what we published.
03 · Node app

Inside a device

Every platform runs the same Rust core, wrapped in a thin shell that fits the operating system. The core only ever starts our bundled inference engine with our signed models. It runs no containers and no customer code, which keeps it safe to install and simple to get through app-store review.

DESKTOP & LAPTOP

Tray app

  • Signed installers: .msi, .dmg, .deb / AppImage
  • Starts at login, updates silently
  • One-line install for servers: curl … | sh
  • Status, earnings and a pause button in the tray
ANDROID

Background service

  • Runs only while charging, on Wi-Fi, screen off
  • Stops when the phone gets warm
  • Restarts by itself after a reboot
  • Serves embeddings, transcription, small models
  • Uber-style job offers, or auto-approve
IPHONE

Charge Mode

  • iOS blocks constant background work
  • Works while plugged in, mostly overnight
  • Or with the app open on a nightstand
  • Lower output per phone than Android
  • Uber-style job offers, or auto-approve
04 · Onboarding

Benchmark and device classes

Right after install, every phone and computer runs a short benchmark (about 2–4 minutes; phones do it on the charger). The result puts the device in a class. The class decides which models it downloads and which jobs it is offered, so a phone is never sent a job built for a gaming PC.

Result screen

STAYING ACCURATE

Re-benchmark

  • Once a month, while idle
  • When live job speed drifts more than 20% from the benchmark

ROUTING

How the class is used

  • Jobs are offered only to classes allowed to run them
  • Within a class: rank by live speed, reputation and distance
  • Server checks results against known ranges for that hardware; a device claiming a higher class than it delivers is moved down

06 · Offers

Job offers, Uber-style

Owners who turn off auto-approve get every job as an offer card, like a ride request: the earnings in large type, the time it takes, and a 30-second countdown. Accept and the job runs right away; decline or let it time out and it goes to the next best device in the same class. Offers only go to devices that are ready at that moment, and the card never shows what the job contains.

ModeWhat happens
Auto-approve (default)Every job the device's class allows runs without asking. One earnings summary a day.
Auto-approve with limitsRuns automatically within the owner's limits: longest job, lowest pay per job, lowest pay per hour, only while charging, only on Wi-Fi. Anything outside the limits arrives as an offer card.
Ask for every jobEvery job arrives as an offer card with a 30-second countdown.
ON THE CARD

What the owner sees

  • Earnings for this job, in large type
  • Equivalent pay per hour
  • Job category and size (e.g. "Transcription · 12 min of audio")
  • Estimated time on this device
  • Countdown; today's total and job count at the top
  • Never the prompt, the output or the customer
THE NUMBERS

How they are worked out

  • Time: job size ÷ this device's benchmark speed, plus model load time if needed
  • Earnings: job price × owner share (80%)
  • Per hour: earnings ÷ estimated time
  • Final credit uses actual work; the receipt shows estimate and final
RULES

Like a driver app

  • Go online / go offline toggle on the home screen
  • Offer goes to one device at a time, best match first
  • Declining never lowers reputation; the scheduler learns which job types an owner prefers
  • Real-time chat goes only to auto-approve devices
  • Desktops get the same card as a tray pop-up

Offers reach phones as high-priority notifications with Accept and Decline buttons (Firebase on Android, time-sensitive Apple Push on iPhone), and as a full-screen card when the app is open. On iPhone, offers are sent only while the phone is in Charge Mode.

07 · Accountability

Dependability and penalties

Declining an offer is always free. Once a device accepts a job, finishing it is the owner's responsibility. If the owner comes back to the device, it stops taking new jobs but finishes the one it is running. Every device has a dependability score from 0 to 100; new devices start at 70. Stopping a job midway costs money and score, depending on the cause.

0–29Suspended · payouts frozen
30–4930-day hold · 0.70× pay
50–6914-day hold · 0.85× pay
70–897-day hold · 0.95× pay
90+3 days · 1.0×

Bands drawn to scale on the 0–100 score. New devices start at 70. Lower bands also get fewer and lower-value offers: 50–69 batch jobs only, 30–49 low-value batch only.

Why the job stoppedExamplesPenalty
Owner actionApp quit, "Stop now", device shut down or put to sleep, went offline, network turned offJob pay forfeited · debit 2× job value · −10 score
Accepted, not deliveredNo first token in time, missed the deadlineForfeit · debit 1× · −8
Device or connection failureWi-Fi drop, crash, out of memory, heat or low-battery cut-offForfeit · −5 · first 2 a week not debited, then 1×
Wrong resultFailed test prompt or re-run mismatchForfeit · debit 3× · −15
Not the owner's faultOur outage, known bug in our app, customer cancelledNo penalty · paid for work done
HOW IT'S COLLECTED

Penalty tools

  • Forfeit: the stopped job pays nothing
  • Debit: from held earnings first, then the balance; a negative balance is paid off before any payout
  • Hold: longer earnings hold at lower scores
  • Earning potential: lower pay multiplier, fewer offers
  • Suspension: 3 owner aborts in 24 h = 24 h off; repeat offenders removed
WINNING IT BACK

Recovery

  • +1 per job finished on time (+0.5 under 1 min), up to +5 a day
  • +3 for every 50 jobs in a row without an abort
  • Penalties count half after 30 days, drop off after 90
FAIR TO OWNERS

Protections

  • "Finish current job, then go offline" never costs anything
  • Warning before quitting: "Quitting now costs $0.08 and 10 points"
  • Every penalty shows the job, cause and evidence, with one-tap appeal
  • First 7 days: device failures are never debited
08 · Runtime

Life of a request

One customer request, start to finish. If the device drops part-way, the scheduler re-sends the job to another device or to rented GPUs, and the customer just sees the answer.

The receipt in step 6 is the device's digital signature over the job ID, model, a fingerprint of the output, token counts and time. It is what the device gets paid on.

10 · Safety

Trust and privacy

CORRECT RESULTS

Checking the work

  • Test prompts with known answers
  • 2–5% of jobs re-run on a second device
  • Reputation score per device
  • New or low-score devices get batch work only, paid after 7 days
REAL DEVICES

Stopping fake nodes

  • One key per device, one pairing per device
  • Paid for verified work, never for just being online
  • Play Integrity on Android, App Attest on iPhone
  • Identity check at payout
CUSTOMER DATA

Privacy

  • Every job runs in a secure sandbox the owner can't look into
  • Jobs are encrypted to the sandbox's own key
  • Customers pick a minimum privacy level per request (see Secure job sandbox)
11 · Privacy

Secure job sandbox

Every job runs inside a sealed sandbox on the device. The job arrives encrypted with a key that only the sandbox holds, so the owner, the app's own interface and our relay servers see only metadata: category, size, time and earnings. The prompt and the answer never touch the disk and are wiped from memory when the job ends.

ConfidentialAndroid phones with protected virtual machines (Android Virtualization Framework, e.g. recent Pixels); partner servers with confidential-computing GPUs (NVIDIA H100 / Blackwell) and CPUs (AMD SEV-SNP, Intel TDX)Hardware-isolated memory with remote attestation. The owner cannot read the job even with full admin or root access.All jobs, including those marked private
SealediPhones; Android phones passing strong Play Integrity; Macs with System Integrity Protection onLocked-down OS sandbox, no debugger access, encrypted job, attestation that the device is not jailbroken or rooted. CUSTOMER CONTROL

Minimum privacy per request

  • API field privacy: confidential, sealed or sandboxed
  • The scheduler sends the job only to devices at that level or higher
  • Higher levels cost more per token
IN THE SANDBOX

Lock-down rules

  • Only our signed engine and signed models run
  • Network only to our relay; no other outbound access
  • Swap off or encrypted; no crash dumps with job data
  • Sandbox refuses to start if the device is rooted, jailbroken or debugged
PROOF

Attestation

  • Sandbox makes a fresh key per job and proves it runs our signed code
  • Android: key attestation and Play Integrity; iPhone and Mac: App Attest; servers: GPU and CPU attestation reports
  • No valid proof, no job

13 · Research

Inference engines and on-device research

The node app is not tied to one engine. Each engine plugs in through the same adapter, and the install benchmark tests every engine available on the device and picks the fastest one per model. Recent on-device work, smolmaxxing in particular, lets ordinary phones run far bigger models than their RAM suggests.

SMOLMAXXING · MIT · ADDED TO REPO AT third_party/smolmaxxing

35B models on phones, by paging experts from flash

Launch thread

1/ Your laptop, desktop and phone sit idle ~20 hours a day. We're building Infera: a network where idle devices serve AI inference and their owners get paid. Install once. It runs when you're away, plugged in and idle. It pauses the moment you're back.

2/ How it works: install the app (Windows / Mac / Linux, Android next, iPhone after). It benchmarks your device and picks the right model. Developers send AI requests through our API. Your device serves them and you earn for each verified job.

3/ Safe by design: it only runs our signed inference engine and signed models. No arbitrary code, no mining.

4/ Developers: OpenAI-compatible API, cheaper batch and embeddings pricing. Free beta credits.

5/ Closed beta opens in waves. Early contributors get a points multiplier. Join the waitlist: [link]. Refer friends to move up.
