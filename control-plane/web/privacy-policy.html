Privacy policy

Data fiduciary / controller: Greencircuit Innovations LLP, India. Last updated: 25 November 2026. Contact for privacy requests: hello@valanor.network

This policy explains what personal data Infera collects, why, how long we keep it, and your rights. For how AI requests themselves are handled, see Data and privacy.

1. What we collect

  • Account data: your email address and an API or contributor key (we store only a hash of each key).
  • Customer usage: for each request, the model, token counts or audio seconds, price, timing, and which device served it. We don't store prompts, audio or answers of live requests. Batch API requests and results are stored so you can fetch them, and deleted 7 days after the job finishes.
  • Contributor device data: device name, platform (for example "android 15"), benchmark results (speed, memory, processor model), a hash of the device's own machine ID (to stop duplicate accounts), job history, dependability score, earnings and penalties.
  • Payout data: your USDT wallet address and network, transaction hashes, and, above the identity-check threshold, the identity details you provide.
  • Waitlist and referrals: email, optional X (Twitter) handle, and referral counts, if you join the waitlist.
  • Technical data: server logs with request method and path (no request bodies or keys), kept by our hosting provider for a limited period.

2. Why we use it (purpose and legal basis)

  • To provide the service: run requests, bill customers, credit and pay contributors (performance of our contract with you).
  • To keep the network honest and secure: dependability scores, duplicate-device checks, fraud and abuse prevention (legitimate interests).
  • To meet legal obligations: tax and accounting records, sanctions screening, identity checks above the payout threshold (legal obligation).
  • To send service messages and, if you joined the waitlist, invitations (consent, which you can withdraw).

We don't sell personal data, use it for advertising, or train AI models on your requests.

3. Who we share it with

  • Contributors' devices receive the content of the request they run (encrypted for that device), never your email or account details.
  • Service providers that host and run Infera for us: Railway (servers and database), GitHub (app downloads), Hugging Face (model downloads by contributors' devices), and, when no device is free, a rented AI cloud provider that runs the request.
  • Payout and compliance: blockchain networks (wallet addresses and transfers are public on-chain), and authorities where the law requires.
  • A buyer or successor if Infera is reorganised or sold, under this policy.

Some providers are outside India (for example in the United States). We use providers that protect data under contract and as required by applicable law.

4. How long we keep it

  • Account, usage, earnings and payout records: while your account is open and for up to 8 years after, for tax and accounting.
  • Batch requests and results: 7 days after the job finishes.
  • Identity-check documents: as long as the law requires, then deleted.
  • Waitlist data: until you join or ask us to remove it.

5. Storage on your device

The website stores your keys and display preferences (for example light or dark theme) in your browser's local storage so you stay signed in. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies. The Infera app keeps its device key in your phone's or computer's secure key store.

6. Security

Keys are stored as hashes; device keys are kept in the operating system's key store; requests are encrypted end to end to one device; payouts need a signed wallet proof. No system is perfectly secure: tell us at once if you think your key or account has been compromised.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the EU/UK GDPR), you can ask to access, correct, or delete your personal data, withdraw consent, object to some processing, and nominate someone to exercise your rights. Email hello@valanor.network; we'll reply within 30 days. We may keep some records where the law requires (for example tax records). You can also complain to your data protection authority, including the Data Protection Board of India.

8. Children

Infera is not for anyone under 18, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

9. Changes

We'll post updates here with a new date and, for significant changes, tell you in the dashboard or app.
